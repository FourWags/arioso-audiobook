Scope and privacy
- Opt-in car browser supports Continue, Authors, Series and Books, dynamic root-tab limits, direct standalone books, series reading order, bounded browse groups and title/author/series search. Author, series and book media IDs are opaque hashes. The media session supports voice search, prepare, resume, pause, stop, seek, rewind and fast forward. A missing file reports a relink message.
- Disabled Android Auto access or Private playback denies new browser connections and suppresses browse/search results. The phone app discloses that trusted car system apps and Google Assistant may receive local book metadata when the user opts in.
- Exact signed APK has no Internet permission. AndroidX Media 1.8.0 adds an app-owned DYNAMIC_RECEIVER_NOT_EXPORTED_PERMISSION; the packaged declaration was verified signature-protected. Other permissions remain only media foreground service permissions. Commercial input audit passed: MIT, Apache 2.0, component and privacy notices match; no bundled audiobook, book cover, external font or native library. Reviewed AndroidX notification PNGs are byte-pinned in the audit.

Limits
- The emulator media commands used synthetic metadata and checked selection, voice search and reported playback actions. It did not play audio through an Android Auto head unit. A parked-car or Desktop Head Unit test on the exact signed APK remains necessary for car UI, voice recognition, steering-wheel buttons, route changes and interruptions.
- The Pixel app was not updated during preparation. Its earlier Downloads-only picker report, personal audiobook playback, live TalkBack/Switch Access, Bluetooth, long playback, physical battery/performance and Android Auto behavior remain unqualified for this candidate.
