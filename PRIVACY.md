# Arioso privacy policy

**Developer:** Four Wags Software  
**App:** Arioso (`app.arioso.audiobook`)  
**Last updated:** 28 October 2026

Arioso is an offline audiobook player. It has no app account, advertising, analytics, crash-reporting SDK, or app Internet permission. Four Wags Software does not receive your library, listening activity, audio, or device data from the app.

## Files and on-device data

Arioso opens audio files, folders, covers, and optional companion PDFs that you choose through Android's document picker. It reads audio metadata and selected cover art to build your library and play your files. With a selected folder grant, Arioso can find a small cover image beside audio; it does not copy the original image. PDFs are rendered inside Arioso and decoded audio is briefly inspected on device only if you turn on Skip silence for a book. The files remain with their original storage provider. Android grants Arioso access to the documents you select; you can revoke access in Android settings or remove a listing in Arioso.

Arioso stores your library listings, edited metadata, listening positions, bookmarks, character and place notes, chapter markers, Favorites/Listen Later/Archive choices, selected companion-PDF links, preferences, listening history, and recent undo or recovery state in its private app storage. Optional listening-time statistics are off until you enable them and can be reset in Arioso. Artwork may be cached in memory while the app runs. Recent seek places are kept only while the current book is loaded and can be cleared in the player. Playback controls and the optional home-screen widget can show book details; Arioso offers a private-playback setting that hides those details from media controls and the widget.

If you explicitly enable Android Auto access, its media browser may receive local book titles and authors and control playback on a connected car display. Private playback disables the browser. Arioso does not transmit this data to Four Wags Software or a network service. The Android document picker and your chosen file provider operate under their own privacy terms. Arioso requests local documents and rejects unsupported remote provider authorities, but the provider controls its own handling of the files.

## Export, retention, and deletion

If you choose to export a library backup or catalog, Arioso writes it to the location you select through Android's document picker. A full backup can contain library metadata, companion-PDF links, and listening state; optional password protection is available. You control that exported file and should keep it private. Arioso does not upload it. You can clear listening history and reset optional listening time inside Arioso. The catalog-only export excludes listening positions, bookmarks, book notes, and character or place notes. Removing a listing or clearing Arioso's app storage removes Arioso's local record; original audio, PDFs, and any backups you exported remain where you put them. Android automatic backup is disabled for Arioso.

## Contact and changes

Privacy questions can be submitted at <https://github.com/FourWags/arioso-audiobook/issues>. Changes to this policy will be dated here and included in future app copies.
